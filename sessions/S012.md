## Mathematical starting point

S011 derives (on paper, with exact independent rational tests) that
a strictly positive finite size-biased priority permutation is
*deletion consistent*: filtering out any discarded subset leaves
the same priority law on the retained vertices. Conditionally on the
first vertex v, the remaining order on V\\N[v] has this law.
In particular, the weighted-permutation pushforward under greedy
acceptance satisfies the first-choice recursion for every output
target, hence agrees with the recursive finite law.

The actual `List.permutations` unique-head enumeration and **full
weighted `orderMass` normalization** are formally proved in S011,
in imported Lean modules, at SHA a613bb87 (PR CI 41196560831).
They close discrete normalisation, not the law-level pushforward.

The S010 fuel and blocked-vertex skip theorems are already imported.
